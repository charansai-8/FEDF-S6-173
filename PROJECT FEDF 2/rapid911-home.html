Workflow

From call to scene in minutes

A streamlined five-step process ensures the right unit reaches every emergency as fast as possible.

01
Call received
Dispatcher logs emergency type, location, and caller details into the form.
02
Priority assigned
System auto-classifies severity and flags critical calls for immediate action.
03
Unit matched
Nearest available ambulance and crew are identified on the live map.
04
Dispatched
Crew receives alert, unit status updates, and route is tracked in real time.
05
Incident closed
Outcome is logged, unit cleared, and report auto-generated for records.
